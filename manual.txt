1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW & USER ROLES
================================================================================
UniCore LMS is a unified academic administration platform built for higher 
education institutions. It provides real-time governance over degree programs, 
course catalogs, term schedules, student candidate enrollment, grading schemes, 
and attendance.

The system supports three distinct roles:
1. Administrator (Registrar / Dean): Full governance over academic semesters, 
   curriculum courses, university departments, faculty assignments, student 
   admissions, domain migrations, and security credentials.
2. Faculty (Professor / Lecturer): Manages assigned course sections, evaluates 
   quizzes/midterm/final examinations, marks attendance, and broadcasts lecture 
   announcements.
3. Student: Browses active courses, registers during open enrollment windows, 
   monitors attendance percentages, reviews grades, and tracks overall CGPA.

2.0 DYNAMIC SYSTEM ANALYTICS & METRICS FILTERING (DEPARTMENT & SEMESTER)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Located in the "Analytics" tab of the Admin Dashboard.
The Analytics header bar features a responsive multi-criteria Filter Bar positioned 
directly beneath the institutional title and above the top KPI metric cards.

A. FILTER CONTROLS:
   1. "Filter Department" (Dropdown):
      - Default option: "All Departments" (value: 'ALL').
      - Dynamically populated from all active university departments (Computer Science, 
        Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence & Data, etc.).
   2. "Filter Semester" (Dropdown):
      - Default option: "All Semesters" (value: 'ALL').
      - Dynamically populated with all configured active semesters in the system 
        (e.g., Fall 2026 (Semester 5), Spring 2026 (Semester 4), Fall 2025 (Semester 3)).
   3. "Reset Filters" (Button):
      - Appears strictly when either Department or Semester is set to a non-default value.
      - Clicking resets both dropdowns back to "All" simultaneously.

B. MULTI-CRITERIA FILTERING BUSINESS RULES:
   1. Default State (Dept = 'ALL', Sem = 'ALL'):
      - Aggregates live institutional figures across the entire university.
   2. Department Only Selected (Dept != 'ALL', Sem = 'ALL'):
      - Enrolled Students, Total Courses, Registrations, Avg Attendance, Avg GPA, 
        and Grade Distribution filter strictly to that department across all semesters.
      - "Faculty Members" card displays the count of faculty affiliated with that department.
   3. Semester Only Selected (Dept = 'ALL', Sem != 'ALL'):
      - Enrolled Students, Total Courses, Registrations, Avg Attendance, Avg GPA, 
        and Grade Distribution filter strictly to that semester cohort.
      - CRITICAL RULE: Faculty members are not directly bound to a specific semester. 
        In this case, the semester filter is IGNORED for the Faculty Members card, 
        and the full university faculty count is shown with subtext: "Across all departments (semester agnostic)".
   4. Both Filters Selected (Dept != 'ALL', Sem != 'ALL'):
      - Metrics filter to the intersection of the selected department and semester.
      - CRITICAL RULE: Faculty Members count depends ONLY on the selected department 
        (the semester filter remains ignored for faculty).

C. VISUAL COMPONENTS DYNAMICALLY UPDATED:
   1. Top 6 Metric Cards:
      - Enrolled Students, Faculty Members, Total Courses, Registrations, 
        Avg Attendance, and Avg Univ GPA update with live calculations and dynamic scope subtexts.
   2. University Academic Grade Distribution:
      - Bell curve bar chart re-calculates grade distribution and percentage of 
        students in Good Academic Standing (CGPA >= 2.00) based on filtered dataset.
   3. Departmental Enrollments & Cohort Breakdown:
      - When Dept = 'ALL': Displays comparative student and course headcounts across all departments.
      - When Dept != 'ALL' and Sem = 'ALL': Displays a semester-by-semester breakdown for the selected department.
      - When Dept != 'ALL' and Sem != 'ALL': Displays detailed cohort metrics (active students, term courses, registrations, cohort GPA).
   4. Attendance Audit Health:
      - Progress bar dynamically recalculates compliance: Eligible (>= 75%), Warning (60-74%), and Debarred (< 60%).

D. ZERO-STUDENT COHORT & EMPTY FILTER BEHAVIOR (SYSTEM INTEGRITY):
   When a department or semester filter has 0 enrolled students (e.g., selecting 
   "Mathematics & Natural Sciences" where no students have registered yet):
   1. Enrolled Students Card: Displays "0" with subtext identifying the zero cohort.
   2. Avg Attendance Card: Displays "0%" with neutral subtext "No student records". 
      The green "> 75% threshold" highlight is suppressed to avoid false compliance indicators.
   3. Avg Univ GPA Card: Displays "0.00" with subtext "No enrolled students", eliminating 
      static university-wide fallback averages.
   4. Attendance Audit Health: Displays "0% (No Cohort)" badge, explicitly states 
      "No enrolled students found in current filter scope to evaluate", and renders a 
      neutral track bar with 0% Eligible, 0% Warning, and 0% Debarred.
   5. Standard Grading Bell Curve: Shows 0 total graded enrollments and "No graded evaluations for this scope"
      (eliminating hardcoded standing percentages).

2.1 ACADEMIC SEMESTERS DIRECTORY & BATCH PROMOTION CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Located in the "Courses & Semesters" tab of the Admin Dashboard.
The system supports up to 8 configured semesters (e.g., Semester 1 through 
Semester 8), representing standard four-year undergraduate degree cycles.

Each semester record tracks:
- Semester Number (1 to 8, uniquely validated)
- Semester Title (e.g., Fall 2026, Spring 2026, Fall 2025)
- Term Code (e.g., FA26, SP26, FA25)
- Duration (Start Date and End Date)
- Registration Window Status (Interactive Open / Closed Toggle)
- Configured Course Count
- Batch Promotion:
  * "Declare Results & Promote" (Blue button): Opens the batch promotion modal 
    to audit curriculum courses, calculate GPA, tag backlogs, and promote students.
  * "● Results Published" (Green badge): Indicates results are published; 
    provides a direct "View Promotion Report" link to inspect evaluated outcomes.
- Actions: Delete semester (with confirmation safety guard).

Note: The obsolete "Current Term" column has been removed. Course registration permissions 
are managed strictly and reliably on a per-semester basis via the "Registration Window" toggle.

2.2 UNDERSTANDING THE "REGISTRATION WINDOW" TOGGLE BUTTON
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Course enrollment permissions are governed directly per-semester in the Semesters Directory table:

A. "REGISTRATION WINDOW" TOGGLE BUTTON:
   - Where to find it:
     In the "Registration Window" column of each semester row in the Semesters 
     Directory table.
   - Visual Appearance:
     * When Open: Green pill button labeled "● Open (Toggle)" with an active pulsing dot.
     * When Closed: Rose/Red pill button labeled "● Closed (Toggle)".
   - What it does:
     Clicking this button toggles course enrollment permissions for that specific 
     semester between OPEN and CLOSED.
   - Effect on the system:
     * When OPEN: Students enrolled in or eligible for this semester can register 
       for, add, or drop courses in their Student Portal during active intake.
     * When CLOSED: The enrollment window is locked. Students viewing this semester 
       in their portal will see a clear "Registration Window Closed" notice. All "Register" 
       and "Drop" action buttons are disabled to prevent unauthorized changes 
       after institutional deadlines.
   - Typical usage:
     Turn it OPEN at the beginning of an academic term or add/drop period. Turn it 
     CLOSED once the deadline passes to freeze course rosters and finalize records.

2.3 ACADEMIC COURSES DIRECTORY & FILTERING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Located immediately below the Semesters Directory. This section manages all course 
offerings, classroom allocations, credit hours, and assigned instructors.

A. "FILTER SEMESTER:" DROPDOWN
   - Displays "All Configured Semesters" plus only the semesters that are 
     actually created and visible in the Semesters Directory table.
   - Shows the live count of courses offered in each semester (e.g., "Fall 2026 (Semester 5) - 5 courses").
   - Choosing a semester filters the catalog to show only courses taught in that term.

B. "FILTER DEPARTMENT:" DROPDOWN
   - Displays "All Departments" plus each university department (e.g., Computer 
     Science (CS), Software Engineering (SE), Electrical Engineering (EE)).
   - Shows the live count of courses under each department.
   - Can be combined with the Semester Filter for multi-criteria slicing.

C. "RESET" BUTTON
   - Appears automatically whenever a filter (Semester, Department) or search 
     query is active.
   - Clicking "Reset" clears all active filters and restores the full course view.

D. SEARCH BOX
   - Located on the right side of the filter bar.
   - Real-time search across course codes (e.g., "CS-301"), course titles (e.g., 
     "Operating Systems"), assigned professor names, and department titles.
   - Includes a quick-clear (x) button when text is entered.
   - Engineered with a non-collapsible minimum width to remain fully readable 
     on all screen resolutions.

E. ADDING & EDITING COURSES
   - Click "+ Add Course" to open the course creation modal.
   - "Target Academic Semester" is a dropdown strictly populated from configured 
     semesters in the directory.
   - "Semester Number" is automatically synchronized and locked to the selected 
     semester, preventing mismatched data entries.
   - "Course Type": Required dropdown with two options:
     * Theory: 3 Credit Hours standard. Strictly enforced validation requiring exactly 
       2 scheduled days (e.g., Monday & Wednesday or Tuesday & Thursday).
     * Lab: 1 Credit Hour standard. Strictly enforced validation requiring exactly 
       1 scheduled day (e.g., Friday).
   - "Class Schedule & Timings": Interactive weekday slot selector (Monday through 
     Saturday) with an "Open Time" dropdown for each day. Selecting a time slot marks 
     that day as active. Includes live validation counters, preset options, and real-time 
     schedule preview.
   - Select the responsible academic department, credit hours, room/venue, maximum student 
     capacity, and appointed faculty instructor. Fully synchronized with MongoDB.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    - Selected via an interactive calendar date picker with current date pre-selected by default.

3.1 Student Creation & Onboarding Rules:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When an administrator enrolls a new student candidate in the "Student Directory" tab:
1. Fresh Admission (Standard Intake):
   - Admission Type is selected as "Fresh Admission".
   - The starting semester is automatically set to "Semester 1" and locked.
   - The UI disables semester selection with a lock indicator to enforce degree 
     entry integrity.
   - Academic status defaults to "active".
2. Transfer / Lateral Entry:
   - Admission Type is selected as "Transfer / Lateral Entry".
   - The administrator selects the starting semester based on recognized transfer credits.
   - The semester dropdown displays ONLY the semesters currently available in the 
     university system (e.g. if only 3 semesters exist, only those 3 are displayed, 
     preventing selection of non-existent terms).
   - Academic status defaults to "active".
3. Session (Enrollment Year) via Calendar:
   - The enrollment year is selected through an interactive calendar date picker 
     (today's date selected by default).
   - The 4-digit session year is extracted directly from the calendar date.
   - The roll number and official institutional email automatically derive from the 
     extracted year and update in real-time with live preview badges.
   - The same calendar picker is available when updating existing students ("Manage & Reset").

3.2 Automated Semester Promotion Engine (Credit-Hour Progression & Backlogs):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Trigger:
- Located in the "Courses & Semesters" tab under the "Academic Semesters Directory".
- In the "Batch Promotion" column of each term, click the blue "Declare Results & Promote" button.
- Once executed, the button changes to a green badge "● Results Published" with a 
  clickable "View Promotion Report" link for historical auditing.

Standard University Credit-Hour System Principles:
- Traditional full-semester detention ("year-back" or forcing students to repeat an 
  entire semester) has been replaced by a modern credit-hour backlog progression model.
- Students advance sequentially to the next semester (`current_semester += 1`), while 
  uncleared or unattempted courses are tagged as course-level backlogs ("repeat courses") 
  that students must clear before degree completion.

Step-by-Step Batch Evaluation Workflow:
1. Course Audit & Curriculum Reconciliation:
   - Queries all configured curriculum courses for that specific semester and department.
   - For every enrolled student currently in that semester, the engine audits their 
     registered/evaluated courses against the offered curriculum:
     * Passed Courses (Grade >= 50% / Grade points > 0): Earned credit hours and quality 
       points are added to cumulative credits and GPA. If the course was previously a 
       backlog, it is resolved and cleared.
     * Failed Courses (Grade F / <50%): Flagged as `status: 'failed'` / `reason: 'failed'` 
       and added to the student's persistent `backlogCourses` array.
     * Unattempted / Missed Courses: Any curriculum course from that semester that the 
       student never registered for is identified and flagged as `status: 'missing'` / 
       `reason: 'missed'`, and added to the student's `backlogCourses` array.
2. Sequential Semester Progression & Academic Standing:
   - For Continuing Semesters (< Semester 8):
     * Increment `current_semester += 1` (e.g., Semester 1 -> Semester 2).
     * Academic Standing:
       - If Cumulative CGPA >= 2.00: Set status to "active" (Good Academic Standing).
       - If Cumulative CGPA < 2.00: Set status to "probation" (Academic Probation). 
         The candidate advances to the next term, but receives an official probation 
         advisory requiring grade improvement and retake prioritization.
   - For Final Term (Semester 8 Graduation Audit):
     * Conferred Graduation: If all required credit hours (>= 130 credits) are earned 
       AND zero pending backlogs exist, academic status is updated to "graduated".
     * Graduation Deferred: If the student reaches Semester 8 but carries uncleared 
       backlogs or missing credits, their status remains "active" (pending graduation) 
       at Semester 8 until all repeat courses are cleared.
3. Interactive Evaluation Report Modal:
   - High-level KPI summary cards:
     * Total Evaluated candidates
     * Promoted (Active) count
     * Promoted on Probation count
     * Students Carrying Backlogs count
     * Graduated count & Pending Graduation count
   - Candidate Filtering Tabs: "All", "Promoted", "On Probation", "Carrying Backlogs", "Graduated".
   - Detailed Roster Table:
     * Student Candidate: Avatar, name, roll number, and department.
     * Term GPA: Evaluated semester GPA.
     * Cumulative CGPA: Overall university CGPA.
     * Progression Transition: Visual transition badge (e.g., Sem 1 → Sem 2 or Sem 8 → Graduated).
     * Academic Standing: Color-coded badge (Active, Academic Probation, Graduated, Pending Graduation).
     * Backlogs / Repeat Courses: List of uncleared courses with specific badges (Failed vs Missed).
     * Academic Justification: Detailed institutional note explaining the progression outcome.
4. Automated Electronic Notifications:
   - Each evaluated candidate receives an official electronic notification detailing 
     their evaluated GPA, cumulative CGPA, updated semester number, academic standing, 
     and an itemized list of any backlog courses requiring re-enrollment.

3.3 Super Admin Manual Override & Audit Trail:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In the "Student Directory" tab, each student candidate card features an 
"Override Semester" administrative button.
Conditions & Requirements:
1. Role-Based Access Control:
   - Strictly restricted to Super Admin or Academic Controller roles.
2. Dynamic Curriculum Semesters:
   - The Target Semester dropdown only displays available semesters configured in the 
     system, rather than hardcoded 1-8 options.
3. Session (Enrollment Year) & Credential Synchronization:
   - Includes an interactive calendar date picker (with current date or student's session date selected).
   - The 4-digit year is extracted from the calendar date and dynamically syncs the 
     student's official Roll Number and institutional email with a live preview.
4. Mandatory Audit Reason:
   - The administrator must supply a non-empty, detailed justification in the confirmation 
     modal (e.g., "Credit transfer evaluation from accredited institution", 
     "Grade rechecking correction approved by Academic Council", "Official medical exemption").
   - Empty or whitespace reasons are strictly rejected by both client-side validation 
     and backend validation middleware.
5. Status Synchronization:
   - When manually overriding a student's semester, the system synchronizes academic 
     status (e.g., setting to 'active' and clearing stale restrictions).
6. Immutable Audit Logging:
   - Every manual change generates a persistent audit log tracking:
     * Target student identifier, roll number, and department
     * Performing administrator identifier and name
     * Previous semester value
     * New target semester value
     * Previous status and synchronized new status
     * Mandatory justification text provided
     * Live UTC timestamp
   - All audit events are permanently reviewable in the dedicated "Audit Logs" (Progression Audit Trail) tab in the top navigation.

================================================================================
Students access their personalized dashboard to manage course registrations and 
track academic progress.

5.1 Course Registration (Repeat Backlogs & Regular Offerings):
- Academic Standing Banner:
  * If in Good Standing: Displays confirmation of active standing.
  * If on Academic Probation (CGPA < 2.00): Displays an amber advisory banner 
    recommending that the student prioritize repeating backlog courses to raise their CGPA.

  1. "Pending / Repeat Courses (Backlogs)":

  * System enforces the university maximum credit limit (18 to 21 credit hours per term).
  * Students can balance repeat courses and regular courses without overloading their term schedule.
- Registration Window Gate:
  * If the semester's Registration Window is OPEN: Students can enroll or drop courses.
  * If CLOSED: Registration actions are locked with an explicit "Registration Window Closed" notice.

5.2 Attendance, Lecture Schedule, & Announcements:
- View assigned lecture schedules, timings, and classroom venues.

6. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ) & TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

Q1: What happens if I close the Registration Window for a semester?
A1: Students will not be able to add new courses or drop existing courses in 
    that semester. Their enrolled courses remain intact and faculty can still 
    grade them. Open the window whenever enrollment or add/drop changes are permitted.

Q2: How does the new Credit-Hour Backlog & Promotion system work instead of full detention?
A2: Under the standard University Credit-Hour System, students are not held back 
    in the same semester ("detained") due to individual failed or missed courses. 
    Instead, all continuing students advance sequentially to the next semester 
    (`current_semester += 1`). Uncleared courses are recorded as course-level backlogs 
    that the student retakes during open registration. If their CGPA is below 2.00, 
    they advance under Academic Probation.

Q3: How do students register for their failed or missed backlog courses?
A3: In the Student Portal under "Course Registration", students will see a dedicated 
    "Pending / Repeat Courses (Backlogs)" section at the top of their registration 
    screen. They can click "Register Repeat" on any backlog course while keeping their 
    total term enrollment within the 21 credit hour workload ceiling.

Q4: What causes a student to be placed on Academic Probation?
A4: If a student's Cumulative CGPA drops below 2.00 during batch promotion evaluation, 
    their academic standing is updated to "probation". They advance to the next semester 
    but receive an advisory banner urging them to improve their academic performance 
    and retake backlog courses.

Q5: What if a course search returns no results in the Admin catalog?
A5: Check if you have an active "Filter Semester" or "Filter Department" selected. 
    Click the "Reset" button in the filter bar to clear all constraints and view 
    the full course catalog.
